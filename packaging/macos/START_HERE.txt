Kula World Rerolled @VERSION@ (macOS, Apple Silicon)

1. Move "Kula World Rerolled.app" wherever you like, e.g. Applications.
2. The app is not notarized, so macOS blocks the first start. Either click
   "Open Anyway" in System Settings > Privacy & Security, or run once in Terminal:
     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "Kula World Rerolled.app"
3. Start the app and point it at your own Kula World disc (PAL, SCES-01000, BIN/CUE).
   The disc is never copied. No BIOS dump is needed: the bundled OpenBIOS (MIT) is used.

Saves, settings and the optional HD texture pack live in
~/Library/Application Support/Kula World Rerolled, so updating the app keeps them.

Third-party notices are in licenses/.
